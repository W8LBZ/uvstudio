> 💡 **TL;DR:** Sends a short burst of tones when you press and/or release the talk button.

- **OFF**: nothing sent. Normal for ham use.
- **UP**: sends your DTMF ID when you press PTT.
- **DOWN**: sends it when you release.
- **UP+DOWN**: both.
- **APOLLO**: short "beep" tones at start and end, like the Apollo missions' Quindar tones. Just for fun.
- Some groups use IDs to see who keyed up. If nobody asked you to, leave it off.
