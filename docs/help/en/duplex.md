> 💡 **TL;DR:** Whether you transmit on the same frequency you listen on, or a shifted one.

- **(blank)**: *simplex*. You talk and listen on the same frequency, radio to radio.
- **+**: transmit [Offset](offset.md) MHz *above* the receive frequency.
- **−**: transmit Offset MHz *below* the receive frequency.

Repeaters listen on one frequency and retransmit on another, so you need + or − to use one. The repeater's listing tells you which. In the US, 2 m repeaters usually shift 0.6 MHz and 70 cm repeaters 5 MHz.
