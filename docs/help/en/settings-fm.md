**TL;DR:** The built-in broadcast FM receiver (music and news stations). Receive only. It has nothing to do with ham FM channels.

### FM band
The broadcast band range. **87.5–108 MHz** is used in most of the world. **76–108** and **76–90 MHz** cover Japan, and **64–76 MHz** is the old Eastern European band.

### FM presets
Up to 48 saved broadcast stations, in MHz. A preset outside the selected band is ignored by the radio.
