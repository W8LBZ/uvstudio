**TL;DR:** Labs edition only. A last resort that puts the radio back to factory state: the external memory is rebuilt, then the stock (original) firmware is installed. Your calibration is kept.

- Everything else is erased: channels, settings, logo, firmware slots and apps. Save backups first (Memories, Radio settings, Calibration).
- Pick your exact model. After the first stage, the page asks you to put the radio in **DFU mode** (hold **PTT** while switching on) to install the stock firmware.
- Use it when the radio is badly misconfigured, or before selling or returning it.
