**TL;DR:** Swaps the receive and transmit frequencies of a repeater channel.

- Lets you listen on the repeater's *input*, so you can tell if you could reach the other station directly, without the repeater.
- Only matters when [Duplex](duplex.md) is + or −.
- Usually left off.

Read more: [Amateur radio repeater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amateur_radio_repeater)
