> 💡 **TL;DR:** Which group this channel belongs to when the radio scans.

- Lists **1–24** let you scan only a set of channels, such as your local repeaters or airband.
- **All**: the channel is in every list.
- **—** (none): not in any list, so list scans pass it by.
- Each list can have a short name (3 characters on the radio, such as **RPT** or **AIR**). See [Scan list names](memories-guide.md#scan-list-names).
- To leave a channel in its list but skip it for now, use [Skip](skip.md).
