**TL;DR:** Key sounds, what the side buttons do, and key locking.

### Beep
Beep on every key press.

### F1Shrt
What a short press of side button 1 (the upper side key) does. Pick any action from the list; popular choices are **MONITOR**, **FLASHLIGHT** and **SCAN**. See [what each action does](settings-keys.md#actions).

### F1Long
What a long press of side button 1 (the upper side key) does. Pick any action from the list; popular choices are **MONITOR**, **FLASHLIGHT** and **SCAN**. See [what each action does](settings-keys.md#actions).

### F2Shrt
What a short press of side button 2 (the lower side key) does. Pick any action from the list; popular choices are **MONITOR**, **FLASHLIGHT** and **SCAN**. See [what each action does](settings-keys.md#actions).

### F2Long
What a long press of side button 2 (the lower side key) does. Pick any action from the list; popular choices are **MONITOR**, **FLASHLIGHT** and **SCAN**. See [what each action does](settings-keys.md#actions).

### M Long
What a long press of the **MENU** key does. Pick any action from the list; popular choices are **MONITOR**, **FLASHLIGHT** and **SCAN**. See [what each action does](settings-keys.md#actions).

### KeyLck
Lock the keypad automatically after this long without a key press. **OFF** never locks by itself.

### SetLck
What the lock blocks: only the keys, keys plus side-button actions, keys plus PTT, or all of them.

### SetPTT
**CLASSIC**: hold PTT to talk. **ONEPUSH**: press once to start transmitting, press again to stop. Handy for long talks, but easy to leave on by mistake.

### SetNav
Which arrow keys move through menus. Match your radio: **LEFT/RIGHT** on the UV-K1, **UP/DOWN** on the UV-K5.

### SetKey
RescueOps editions only: the key you hold with PTT at power-on to enter Rescue Ops mode.

### Actions
What the side-button and MENU long-press actions do. Some exist only in certain firmware editions.
- **NONE**: nothing.
- **FLASHLIGHT**: the torch on top of the radio.
- **POWER**: step through transmit power levels. **POWER HIGH**: jump straight to full power.
- **MONITOR**: open the squelch to hear weak or noisy signals.
- **SCAN**: start or stop scanning.
- **VOX**: turn voice-operated transmit on or off.
- **FM RADIO**: the broadcast FM receiver.
- **1750Hz**: send a 1750 Hz tone burst, which opens some older repeaters (mostly in Europe).
- **LOCK KEYPAD**: lock the keys.
- **VFO A/B**: switch between the upper and lower VFO.
- **VFO/MEM**: switch between frequency mode and memory channels.
- **MODE**: change the modulation (FM, AM, USB…).
- **RX MODE**: cycle the **RxMode** setting (single, dual or cross-band listening).
- **MAIN ONLY**: show only the selected VFO.
- **PTT**: use the button as a second talk button.
- **WIDE/NARROW**: switch the channel between wide and narrow FM.
- **MUTE**: silence the speaker.
- **RxA**: cycle the receive audio profile (**SetRxA**).
- **REMOVE OFFSET**: transmit on the receive frequency for now, to talk directly instead of through a repeater.
- **RF LOG**: open the activity history. **BEAM**: send this channel's settings to another radio. **FOX HUNT**, **BEACON**, **TONE CALL**: special tools in some editions.
