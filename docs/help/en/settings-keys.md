**TL;DR:** Key sounds, what the side buttons do, and key locking.

### Beep
Beep on every key press.

### F1Shrt
Action for a short press of side button 1 (the upper side key). Popular picks: **MONITOR** (open squelch to hear weak signals), **FLASHLIGHT**, **SCAN**.

### F1Long
Action for a long press of side button 1.

### F2Shrt
Action for a short press of side button 2 (the lower side key).

### F2Long
Action for a long press of side button 2.

### M Long
Action for a long press of the **MENU** key.

### KeyLck
Lock the keypad automatically after this long without a key press. **OFF** never locks by itself.

### SetLck
What the lock blocks: only the keys, keys plus side-button actions, keys plus PTT, or all of them.

### SetPTT
**CLASSIC**: hold PTT to talk. **ONEPUSH**: press once to start transmitting, press again to stop. Handy for long talks, but easy to leave on by mistake.

### SetNav
Which arrow keys move through menus. Match your radio: **LEFT/RIGHT** on the UV-K1, **UP/DOWN** on the UV-K5.

### SetKey
RescueOps editions only: the key you hold with PTT at power-on to enter Rescue Ops mode.
