> 💡 **TL;DR:** How strongly the radio transmits. Use the lowest level that gets through.

- **HIGH** about 5 W · **MID** about 2 W · **LOW5** 1 W · **LOW4** 500 mW · **LOW3** 250 mW · **LOW2** 125 mW · **LOW1** under 20 mW.
- **USER**: uses the radio's **SetPwr** menu setting, so you can change it on the radio without reprogramming.
- Higher power reaches farther but drains the battery faster and warms the radio.
- Real output varies from radio to radio and band to band.
