**TL;DR:** How far the frequency moves each time you turn the knob or press up/down.

- Common choices: **12.5** or **25** kHz for ham FM, **8.33** kHz for airband, **5** kHz for general tuning.
- The frequency snaps to this step. On VFOs with steps of 10 kHz or more, the radio uses half the step as its grid, so 12.5 kHz gives a 6.25 kHz grid.
- For a fixed memory channel it mostly matters when you tune away from it.
