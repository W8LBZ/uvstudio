> 💡 **TL;DR:** How far your transmit frequency moves away from the receive frequency, in MHz.

- Only used when [Duplex](duplex.md) is + or −. With simplex it's ignored.
- Common US values: **0.6** on 2 m (144–148 MHz) and **5** on 70 cm (420–450 MHz).
- The page outlines in orange a 5 MHz offset on 2 m or 0.6 MHz on 70 cm (usually the other band's value copied by mistake), and + or − with an offset of 0.
- Example: a repeater on 146.940 with − and 0.6 means you transmit on 146.340.
