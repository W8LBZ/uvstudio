> 💡 **TL;DR:** Each band remembers where you left it. These rows are those saved spots, two per band (VFO A and VFO B).

- The radio splits its range into 7 fixed bands (starting at 18, 108, 137, 174, 350, 400 and 470 MHz). You can't change the bands.
- When you tune in frequency mode, the radio keeps what you had set in each band. Switch to another band and back, and you land where you were, with the same offset, tones, power and so on.
- It keeps one saved spot for each band for VFO A and another for VFO B, the radio's two "knobs". That's the 14 rows here.
- A row is one saved spot, not a rule for the whole band. Offset is the repeater split (see [Offset](offset.md)), not the end of a range.
- The radio overwrites these rows as you use it. To keep a frequency for good, store it in a memory channel.
- A VFO frequency must stay inside its band.
