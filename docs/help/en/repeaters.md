**TL;DR:** A repeater is a radio station on a hilltop or tall building that rebroadcasts your signal much farther than your handheld can reach. To use one you need three things from its listing: its frequency, its offset, and its tone.

### How it works
The repeater listens on one frequency (its *input*) and transmits on another (its *output*) at the same time. You listen on its output and transmit on its input.

### Setting up a channel
Take these from the repeater's listing (club website, RepeaterBook, a local band plan):
- **[Frequency](frequency.md)**: the output, for example 146.940.
- **[Duplex](duplex.md)** and **[Offset](offset.md)**: the direction and size of the shift, for example − 0.6.
- **[TX tone](tx-tone.md)**: the access tone, for example 100.0. Many repeaters need it.
- **[Mode](mode.md)**: usually FM. Some use NFM.

### Using it
- Listen first. Press PTT, wait a moment, then talk.
- Give your call sign. Keep transmissions short; many repeaters time out after a few minutes.
- If the repeater doesn't respond, check the TX tone and offset first.
- To see if you could reach someone directly, try [Reverse](reverse.md).
