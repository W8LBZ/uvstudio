**TL;DR:** The boot logo is the picture shown when the radio switches on. You can read the current one or upload your own.

### Read
Press **Read logo from radio** to see the current logo, then download it as a PNG.

### Upload
- Choose any image (PNG, JPG and so on). It's resized to 128 × 64 pixels and turned into black and white.
- Adjust the threshold until the preview looks right. Simple, high-contrast images work best.
- Press **Upload logo to radio**.
- To see it at power-on, the radio's **POnMsg** setting must include the logo (LOGO or ALL).

Changing the logo doesn't affect how the radio works.
